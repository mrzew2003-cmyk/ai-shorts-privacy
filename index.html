AI Shorts Automation

AI Shorts Automation is a personal project that helps its operator prepare and publish short-form videos to their own YouTube channel.

What the app does

  • Assists with preparing short-form video content.
  • Uses Google OAuth to let the operator connect their YouTube account.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos when the operator initiates an upload.

Google account access

The app requests only the Google/YouTube permissions needed for its YouTube upload functionality. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
