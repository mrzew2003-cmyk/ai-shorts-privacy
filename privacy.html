Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Shorts Automation ("the app") handles information when you connect a Google or YouTube account.

Information we access

When you authorize the app, Google may provide access to the YouTube account information and permissions shown on Google's consent screen. The app uses the YouTube permissions it requests to support video-upload functionality.

How information is used

  • To authenticate the account you choose to connect.
  • To upload videos and perform related YouTube actions that you initiate.
  • To maintain the authorization needed for the app to perform those actions.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and related data may be stored locally in the app's operating environment to maintain authorization. Access to these files should be restricted to the app operator. The app does not sell Google user data or share it with third parties for advertising. Data is not shared except as needed to provide the app's stated functionality, comply with law, or protect rights and security.

Data retention and deletion

Authorization data is retained only as long as needed for the app's operation. You can revoke the app's access through your Google Account security settings. To request deletion of information held by the app operator, contact the email below and identify the account or data concerned.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored authorization data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. The updated date will be revised when changes are made.
